# The Great Gathering Spot

This table has seen more rad-roach stew and recycled water than a Brahmin has teeth. It’s where the crew drops their weary gear, swaps stories of close calls with Deathclaws, and shares a rare, lukewarm Nuka-Cola. 

In a world where every shadow hides a scavenger, this table is the one place where nobody has to watch their back. Friends, family, and the occasional wanderer with a good story—they all find a seat here eventually. Just don’t mind the rust; it adds character.
