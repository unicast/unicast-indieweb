# The Sound of the Wastes

ROBCO INDUSTRIES - AUDIO TERMINAL v2.1
---

Welcome to the heart of the studio. This console was salvaged from a pre-war radio station in the ruins of Old Berlin. It's been retrofitted with vacuum tube preamps and custom-wound transformers to capture that warm, vintage saturation that digital just can't replicate.

### Current Rack Setup:
*   **Tube Compressor**: For that heavy, breathing vocal sound.
*   **Tape Delay**: A modified reel-to-reel with a loose belt drive for perfect "wow and flutter".
*   **Ham Radio Transceiver**: Sometimes used as a lo-fi microphone or to broadcast to the local raider frequencies.
