# Mercedes-Benz SLK (R170) - Wasteland Edition

A pre-war luxury roadster that's seen some serious modifications. The retractable hardtop still works, surprisingly, though it squeaks like a dying Rad-rat. 

The paint is mostly rust and desert dust now, and those bullet holes in the rear quarter panel tell a story of a narrow escape from a Raider ambush near the old Autobahn.

It’s not just a car; it’s a ticket out of here when the next dust storm hits.
